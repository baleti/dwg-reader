dwg-viewer controls
Ctrl+F: find text (Enter next, Shift+Enter previous)
M: measure (click points, snaps to vertices, midpoints and intersections, Shift = no snap, double click / Enter finish, Esc clear)
Left drag: select (left to right = window, right to left = crossing)
Click: pick, Shift+click: add/remove, Ctrl+A: all, Esc: clear
Middle/right drag or Space+drag or H: pan; wheel: zoom; F: fit
Double click a viewport: open its model window
Layers: click / Shift range / Ctrl toggle rows, buttons act on the selection
? : show / hide this help
